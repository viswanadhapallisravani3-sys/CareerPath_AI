# 🚀 CareerPath AI

**CareerPath AI** is an AI-based career recommendation project that helps students identify suitable career paths based on their **skills, interests, education, activities, and experience**.

The system analyzes the user's profile, calculates matching scores for different career paths, and recommends the **top 5 suitable career options** along with a career roadmap and missing skills to develop.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students often find it difficult to decide which career path is suitable for them because they may have different skills, interests, educational backgrounds, and experiences.

CareerPath AI aims to provide personalized career guidance by analyzing these factors and suggesting career paths that match the user's profile.

---

## 💡 Objective

The main objectives of CareerPath AI are:

* Analyze a student's skills and interests.
* Match the student's profile with different career paths.
* Calculate a matching score for each career.
* Recommend the top 5 career options.
* Provide a career roadmap.
* Identify missing skills that the student can improve.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🔹 Career Recommendation

Recommends suitable career paths based on the user's profile.

### 🔹 Profile Matching

Considers multiple factors such as:

* Skills
* Interests
* Education
* Activities
* Experience

### 🔹 Matching Score

Calculates a weighted matching score between the user's profile and available career paths.

### 🔹 Top 5 Recommendations

Displays the five career paths with the highest matching scores.

### 🔹 Career Roadmap

Provides a roadmap to help the user understand the steps involved in pursuing a recommended career.

### 🔹 Missing Skills

Identifies skills that the user may need to develop for a particular career path.

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

The system follows these steps:

```text
User Profile
     ↓
Skills & Interests
     ↓
Education, Activities & Experience
     ↓
Profile Matching
     ↓
Career Matching Score
     ↓
Top 5 Career Recommendations
     ↓
Career Roadmap + Missing Skills
```
